STEP 04

Make It Yours

AI creates the draft. You create the value.

📄 AI Raw Draft (Generic & Surface-level)
"Automation is changing the labour market. Some jobs are disappearing, while new ones are emerging, workers need to adapt to new technologies."

YOUR THINKING (What you change) ↓

✨ Your Exemplary Version (Grounded & Thoughtful)
"Automation does not simply make some jobs disappear. It also changes what people do at work. When repetitive tasks are automated, workers spend more time on tasks requiring judgement, creativity or human communication."

Quick Comparison

Aspect Broad AI Output Your Improved Version
Source Unverified claims Fact-checked information combined with your own ideas
Tone Generic and robotic Personal and appropriate for the assignment
Value General overview of automation's impact Original work supported by real-world examples

HOW TO TRANSFORM THE OUTPUT

1. ADD YOUR CONTEXT

Connect answer to assignment rules, real examples, or personal background.

2. PUT IT IN YOUR WORDS

Rewrite ideas so you understand and explain them clearly yourself.

3. CHECK YOUR CLAIMS

Avoid conclusions you cannot support with logic, sources, or facts.
