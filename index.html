Why are some artificial intelligence answers better than others?

> Write an essay about ecology and urban green spaces.

Produces a generic text draft lacking specific academic citations or recent data.

> Explain quantum mechanics like I'm 10 years old.

Delivers an engaging summary using clear, relatable real-world analogies.

> Review my C# code for performance bottlenecks.

Provides a detailed analysis highlighting memory efficiency tips.

> Write a conclusion based on my notes.

Generates a generalized summary that misses your actual research findings.

> Solve this algebra problem with steps.

Gives a final number without logic, making it hard to check or learn.

> Create authentic logo for a sauce shop.
