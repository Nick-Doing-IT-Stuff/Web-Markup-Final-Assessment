STEP 01

Understand the Task

Before asking AI for help, use your brain to break down what you actually need to do.

Let's break down in 5 easy steps:

  • 01 What do I need to deliver?
  • 02 What is the topic of my assignment?
  • 03 What are the requirements?
  • 04 Who is it for?
  • 05 What can AI actually help me with?

Pro tip: this methodology works for essays, coding tasks, research or math problems.

Real Assignment Example:

Write a 2-page analytical essay on the impact of automations on the labour force. The text must include at least 3 arguments with real life examples and be targeted as economics students.
