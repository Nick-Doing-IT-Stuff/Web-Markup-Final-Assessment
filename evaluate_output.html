STEP 03

Evaluate AI Output

Confident doesn't mean correct. Always check before you trust it.

AI-Generated Answer Sample (click underlined text to inspect claims)

"Regular physical activity can improve memory and concentration. found that students who exercise regularly performed on memory tests. Recent studies also suggest that exercise increases neurogenesis in the brain, which "
❓

Which 2023 study? Can you find the original study, dataset, or peer-reviewed publication behind this claim?

❓

Where does this 40% number come from? Isn't this a statistics manipulation or an exaggerated summary?

❓

Is this an oversimplification? Correlation does not equal causation. How strong is this link in reality?

Questions to Ask

1. WHERE IS IT FROM?

Check sources. Find original studies, datasets, or backend references.

2. DOES IT MAKE SENSE?

Check logic. Does evidence genuinely support the final conclusion?

3. WHAT FEELS OFF?

Spot missing details, absolute claims, or confident statements without evidence.

Think Before You Regenerate

Not every problem needs another AI request. If you already have enough information, take a moment to think, check, and work with what you have.

ALSO BEAR IN MIND

Protect your data

Don't share personal, sensitive, or confidential information with AI unless you really need to.

Be responsible

You own the result. AI can help you learn and work, but you are responsible for what you submit.

Save resources

Every request requires electricity and computing resources. Think before you regenerate.
